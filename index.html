Fourier Drawing Machine

A chain of turning circles draws a shape. Move the slider to change the number of circles, N.

Draw a line with your finger or mouse. When you release, the line closes automatically.

The top chain sets the x coordinate. The left chain sets the y coordinate. The pen is where the two dashed lines cross.

Circle k turns k times per lap. Its radius is √(aₖ² + bₖ²). The program takes 1024 points along the original shape. Then it calculates the coefficients from these points.

Try the heart. Its formula has only 4 frequencies, so the error is almost 0 at N = 4. The star has sharp corners, so its error decreases slowly, even at a large N.

Fourier analysis does more than draw. See seven examples beyond drawing: JPEG, MP3, spectrum analysis, heat flow, spectral methods, time series, and the Fourier transform.
